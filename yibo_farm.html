The Art of Stillness

"Yibo Farm is not a business. It is a commitment to the land. In a world that never stops moving, we offer the rare privilege of standing still."

As of 2026, we have completely phased out motorized access. The road ends at our gate. To visit Yibo Farm is to travel on your own two feet, carrying only what you need, and leaving only silence behind.

Our trails connect directly to the heart of the national park, yet they remain hidden from the maps of the modern world. There are no crowds here. No engines. No artificial lights.

We do not accept walk-ins or online bookings. Each visit is personally arranged to ensure the preservation of our agricultural sanctuary.
