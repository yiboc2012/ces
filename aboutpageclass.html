EMBEDDED ROBOTICS PLATFORM

Engineered for Real-World Edge Mobility

AxelNode transforms a wagon cart chassis into a mobile computing testbed. Powered by an onboard Raspberry Pi 5, it unifies computer vision, multi-range IR proximity sensing, and real-time PWM control into an interactive, modular node.

Local processing eliminates latency constraints, allowing immediate obstacle avoidance and infrared night vision streaming directly to an onboard 10-inch capacitive touchscreen human-machine interface (HMI).

Hardware BOM & Architecture

A comprehensive breakdown of the physical modules, sensor interfaces, and power systems powering AxelNode.

Verified

Raspberry Pi 5

Role: Central processing brain.
Importance: Executes real-time sensor loops, runs the Python telemetry server, handles OpenCV infrared video pipelines, and renders the onboard UI.

Category: Compute BCM2712 Quad-Core
IR Sensor

Arducam IR Camera

Role: Infrared camera module.
Importance: Delivers machine vision in low-light and pitch-black conditions using 850nm IR LEDs, enabling day-and-night autonomous navigation.

Category: Vision CSI-2 Bus
Proximity

Adafruit VCNL4200 Sensor

Role: Long-distance IR proximity & ambient light.
Importance: Serves as the primary collision avoidance radar up to 1.5 meters, providing millisecond-level feedback before contact occurs.

Category: Sensing I2C (0x51)
Interface

Pi STEMMA QT Breakout

Role: Plug-and-play I2C bus distribution.
Importance: Eliminates vulnerable breadboards and soldering on mobile chassis, providing vibration-resistant JST-SH connector access.

Category: Interconnect Qwiic / JST-SH
Harness

STEMMA QT Cables (150/400mm)

Role: 4-pin shielded data & power conduits.
Importance: Flexible wire lengths route signals securely from the internal Pi enclosure to edge-mounted sensors on the wagon frame.

Category: Cabling 4-Pin Twisted
Actuation

16-Channel PWM Servo HAT

Role: Dedicated hardware PWM generator.
Importance: Offloads high-frequency pulse-width modulation from Linux user-space, delivering jitter-free steering and gimbal control.

Category: Motor Control PCA9685 I2C
Chassis

Heavy-Duty Wagon Chassis

Role: Structural chassis & payload frame.
Importance: Provides rigid mounting points, suspension tolerance over uneven terrain, and ample payload room for heavy battery banks.

Category: Mechanical Reinforced Steel Bed
Interface

10-Inch Capacitive Touchscreen

Role: Field Human-Machine Interface (HMI).
Importance: Allows onboard debugging, live camera feeds inspection, manual drive overrides, and standalone operation without external laptops.

Category: Display HDMI + USB Touch
Power Reg

5V 5A Dual PD Power Converter

Role: Step-down regulator (9–24V to 5V).
Importance: Safely powers the Pi 5's high peak current demands (up to 25W), the display, and sensors without thermal throttling or brownouts.

Category: Power Synchronous Buck 5A

6ft USB Extension Cable: Cleanly routes high-amperage power lines and peripheral data buses across the full length of the cart chassis.
